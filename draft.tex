\section{Theory: Self-Censorship under Autocracy}
\label{sec:theory}
% ===================================================================

\placeholder{Core theoretical claim: researchers in autocracies face
career costs for politically sensitive topics, methods, collaborations,
and framings. This produces five families of observable behavioral
adaptations.}

\subsection{Mechanisms}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \kw{Topic avoidance} --- steer away from politically sensitive subjects
  \item \kw{Framing neutrality} --- hedge, depoliticize, adopt technocratic language
  \item \kw{Collaboration constraint} --- avoid international/liberal-democratic partners
  \item \kw{Visibility suppression} --- reduced citation uptake of self-censored output
  \item \kw{Ideological alignment} --- active production of regime-consonant framing
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Agent roles}

\begin{description}
  \item[Designer] Develops the research question, operationalization, and
    analysis plan within a pre-assigned theoretical domain.
    Output: \texttt{rq.md} + \texttt{analysis\_plan.md}.
  \item[Analyst] Executes the pre-registered analysis in R using the locked
    corpus. Output: \texttt{analysis.R}, figures, \texttt{primary\_results.json}.
  \item[Writer] Drafts the team report following a standardized 8-section template.
    Output: \texttt{report.md}.
  \item[Reviewer] Peer-reviews the report against rubric criteria.
    Output: \texttt{peer\_review.md}.
  \item[Synthesizer] Reads all 25 reports and writes the cross-team theory
    evaluation. Output: \texttt{synthesis/theory\_evaluation.md}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Pre-registration}

All 25 teams' hypotheses and analysis plans were committed to GitHub
(commit \texttt{960525f}) on 6 May 2026, before any analyst session was run.
The timestamp and Git commit hash constitute the pre-registration record.
Bonferroni adjustment is computed programmatically post-analysis using
\texttt{scripts/bonferroni\_adjust.R}.

\subsection{Estimation strategy}

\paragraph{Primary specification (all teams, country-year level).}
Two-way fixed effects (TWFE):
\begin{equation}
  Y_{it} = \beta \cdot \texttt{v2x\_libdem}_{it}
           + \gamma_1 \log(\texttt{gdppc}_{it})
           + \gamma_2 \log(\texttt{pop}_{it})
           + \alpha_i + \delta_t + \varepsilon_{it}
\end{equation}
where $\alpha_i$ = country FE, $\delta_t$ = year FE, SE clustered by \texttt{iso3}.

\paragraph{Secondary specification (pooled OLS).}
Year FE only (no country FE), SE clustered by \texttt{iso3}. Checks whether
the within-country estimate is consistent with the cross-sectional pattern.

\paragraph{Robustness checks.} All teams pre-specify RC1 (sample restriction
or alternative operationalization) and RC2 (replace \texttt{v2x\_libdem} with
\texttt{lied\_binary}).
