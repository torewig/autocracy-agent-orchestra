# Agent Orchestra v2 — Interim Results Report

**Date:** 2026-06-23
**PI:** Tore Wig, University of Oslo
**Project:** How does autocracy, and type of autocracy, shape the contents, direction, and progress of the social sciences and humanities?
**Unified theory:** Self-censorship — researchers in autocracies avoid politically sensitive topics, methods, collaborations, and framings to minimize career risk.

---

## Executive summary

- **21 of 25 active teams complete** (18 simple/medium + 3 ideological-alignment).
- **The ideological-alignment family (Teams 23–25) is now finished.** All three used a stratified ~152K-abstract sample classified with Claude Haiku (`claude-haiku-4-5`), then the pre-registered two-way fixed-effects specification.
- **No team reaches significance**, either nominally at p<0.05 or after Bonferroni correction within its sub-family. Across all 21 completed teams, **0 of 21** primary tests are significant after family-wise correction.
- **4 teams remain** (05, 06, 10, 12), all expensive LLM/embedding analyses, pending a budget decision. Team 06 (embeddings) is partially complete (~65%) and paused.
